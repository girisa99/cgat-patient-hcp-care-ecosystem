The 'INDIA' regional zone is split into five distinct sub-regions—NORTH (Hindi Belt), SOUTH (Dravidian: Tamil/Telugu/Kannada/Malayalam), WEST (Maharashtra/Gujarat), EAST (Bengal/Odisha), and PAN (Indian English)—to address India's massive linguistic and cultural diversity. All sub-regions utilize Gemini 3 Pro as the primary LLM (strongest Indic language support) and Azure Neural for TTS (10+ Indian voice codes with viseme data). Fallback LLM chains are tailored by sub-region: WEST and PAN prioritize Claude 4 (business/formal tone); NORTH, SOUTH, and EAST prioritize GPT-4o. Key differentiator: 4 Dravidian languages (South India) and Bengali/Odia (East India) are rarely offered by competitors, giving the platform a significant coverage advantage.
