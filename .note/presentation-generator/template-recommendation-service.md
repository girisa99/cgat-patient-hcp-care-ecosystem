# Template Recommendation Service - Dynamic System

## Overview
Fully extensible, context-aware template matching system with no hardcoded limits.

## New Categories Added
- **Investor & VC**: Seed pitch, Series A/B, Investor updates, Due diligence
- **Storytelling**: Case study, Origin story, Vision story, Transformation
- **Training** (with sub-options): Video, Manual, Quick Reference, Workshop, E-Learning, Certification
- **Compliance** (with sub-options): Policy, Audit, Training

## Consulting Frameworks
- McKinsey: 7S, Three Horizons, Influence Model
- BCG: Matrix, Advantage Matrix, Digital Acceleration
- Bain: NPS, Strategy Diamond
- Generic: Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, PESTLE, Value Chain, Balanced Scorecard, Ansoff, Blue Ocean

## Template Styles
- pure-consulting, consulting-hybrid, industry-focused
- creative-narrative, data-analytical, educational
- investor-pitch, storytelling, mixed-adaptive

## Integration
TemplateRecommendationPanel.tsx integrates into Step 2 of PresentationWizard.
